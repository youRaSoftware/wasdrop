## Notes for Review (App Review Information → Notes, на английском)

```
Thank you for reviewing Fruity Drop again. This build addresses the Guideline 4.3(b) feedback on our previous submission: we did not resubmit the same app — the concept has been extended so that the core "drop and merge" loop is only one layer of the game.

What is new since the rejected build:

1. ORDERS. Every game gives the player three simultaneous goals (e.g. "Get a lemon", "4 merges in a row", "Melon within 40 drops"). Completing an order awards a star (and a booster charge for the hard ones). Orders are visible at the top of the game screen from the first second.

2. JARS. Seven jar shapes (vase, bowl, shelf, flask, slope, hourglass, plus the classic) with different physics behaviour; new shapes unlock with stars earned from orders. Menu → "Jar" button.

3. THREE MODES. Classic, Time attack (2 minutes) and Daily challenge (the same seeded fruit queue and orders for every player, one attempt per day). Menu → the two buttons under "Play".

4. GAME CENTER. Three leaderboards (one per mode) and ten achievements. The trophy button appears in the menu after signing in to Game Center.

5. A three-step "How to play" walkthrough on first launch, also available in Settings.

Everything in the app is original: eleven hand-drawn fruit characters with two expressions each, a physics model where fruit bodies are derived from the artwork (elongated fruits roll and settle on their side), three boosters with their own animations, six wallpaper themes, a saved-game system, native iPad layout and full localisation into six languages.

The app has no ads, no accounts, no tracking and no in-app purchases in this version, and works fully offline.

Test tips: the daily challenge allows one attempt per day; to see it again, change the device date. Shaking the device is used by the "Shake" booster after tapping its button.
```
